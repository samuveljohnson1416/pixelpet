PixelPet Focus is a pixel creature that lives on your taskbar, or on your Dock. It watches the window in front of you, and when Shorts or Reels outstay their welcome it walks over, glares, and shuts the tab. The rest of the day it naps, climbs your screen edges and keeps you company.

What it does all day

Every behaviour below runs on your machine, from things your computer already tells it: the title of your window, the app it belongs to, the address in your browser's bar.

patrol

Each site gets its own leash: 6 seconds for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, 90 for Reddit, 4 minutes for ordinary YouTube. Run over the limit and the pet walks across, counts down, and closes that tab. Click it during the countdown and it lets you off.

your windows

It jumps onto title bars and rides along when you drag them, climbs the screen edges, hangs upside-down from the top, and shoves a window aside if you throw it hard enough.

company

It pulls out a laptop while you type, wears headphones when you plug them in, bops to music, grabs popcorn and a drink for movie night, and reacts to your charger.

clipboard

Copy "call mom in 20 min" and it offers to remember it. It also strips tracking junk from links and works out sums. Anything that looks like a password or a one-time code is ignored outright.

growing up

Closed tabs and finished reminders earn XP. It levels up, evolves, unlocks achievements, and writes a one-line memory of each day. A week of focus sessions earns it a pair of study glasses.

tools

Battery, free disk space, memory and uptime in one menu, with a system check and one-click Screenshot, Task Manager (Activity Monitor on a Mac) or lock screen. It warns you the day your disk gets tight.

It watches your coding agents too

Connect an agent in one click and the pet shows a working timer, runs to your cursor when the agent needs permission, and cheers when a task lands.

Install it in three steps

On Windows

  1. Download PixelPetFocus.exe from the latest release and put it somewhere permanent, like C:\Apps\PixelPet.
  2. Double-click it. There is no installer. Windows will warn you about an unsigned app: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Right-click the pet for its menu. Tick Start with Windows if you want it there every morning.

Needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Everything it runs on already ships with Windows.

On a Mac

  1. Download PixelPetFocus-mac.zip from the latest release, open it, and drag PixelPet Focus into Applications.
  2. Open it. It isn't notarized, so the first time macOS stops it: go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Allow it in Accessibility when it asks, and let it control your browser when that prompt appears, so it can read titles and close the right tab. Tick Open at login in its menu.

Needs macOS 12 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. It lives above the Dock; right-click it for the menu.

What it never does

no network

There is no networking code in the app at all. No accounts, no telemetry, no update checks. Unplug your internet and nothing changes.

no keylogging

It notices that you're typing, never which keys. There is no keyboard hook, and nothing you type is read or stored.

nothing saved

Copied text stays in memory and is never written to disk. Settings, XP and reminders live in plain text files you can read and edit yourself.
